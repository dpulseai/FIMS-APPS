Privacy Policy for FIMS Mobile Application

Effective Date: December 1, 2025

Last Updated: December 1, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Field Inspection Management System (FIMS) mobile application ("App"). This App is operated by Zilla Parishad Chandrapur ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application for conducting field inspections and managing inspection data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Name and employee identification
  • Contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Login credentials (username and encrypted password)
  • Department and role information

2.2 Inspection Data

  • Inspection forms and reports
  • Photos and images captured during inspections
  • Location data (GPS coordinates) for inspection sites
  • Date and time stamps of inspections
  • Comments and observations

2.3 Device Information

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • App version and usage statistics
  • Network connection information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate users and manage user accounts
  • To enable field inspection activities and data collection
  • To store and manage inspection records and reports
  • To track inspection locations and maintain geographical records
  • To facilitate offline functionality and data synchronization
  • To generate reports and analytics for administrative purposes
  • To improve the App's functionality and user experience
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements

4. Camera Permission

Our App requires access to your device's camera for the following purposes:

  • To capture photographs during field inspections as documentary evidence
  • To document site conditions, infrastructure, and facilities
  • To attach visual records to inspection reports

Important: Photos taken through the App are used solely for official inspection purposes. We do not use your camera for any other purpose, and you maintain full control over when photos are captured.

5. Location Permission

The App uses location services to:

  • Record the geographical coordinates of inspection sites
  • Enable location-based inspection assignments
  • Provide map-based visualization of inspection locations
  • Verify that inspections are conducted at the correct locations

Location data is collected only when you are actively using the App for inspections.

6. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • Data is stored securely using Supabase cloud infrastructure
  • All data transmissions are encrypted using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Access to data is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security audits and updates are performed
  • Offline data is stored securely on your device using encrypted local storage

However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information and inspection data for as long as necessary to:

  • Fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and record-keeping obligations
  • Maintain historical inspection records as required by government policies

8. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Within the Organization: With authorized government officials and departments who need access for administrative purposes
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulations
  • Service Providers: With trusted service providers (e.g., Supabase) who assist in operating the App, under strict confidentiality agreements

9. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your personal information

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

10. Offline Functionality

The App includes offline functionality to allow inspections in areas with limited connectivity. When working offline:

  • Data is temporarily stored on your device
  • Data is automatically synchronized with our servers when connectivity is restored
  • Offline data is protected using device-level security measures

11. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services, including:

  • Supabase: For backend data storage and authentication
  • Google Maps: For location services and mapping (if applicable)

These third-party services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies.

12. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for use by authorized government employees only and is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Sending a notification through the App (for significant changes)

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

14. Consent

By using the FIMS Mobile App, you consent to the collection, use, and processing of your information as described in this Privacy Policy.
